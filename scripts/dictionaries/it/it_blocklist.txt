# ── Accent misspellings (2026-11-19, maintainer: "fix the Italian unaccented words") ──────────
# Every entry below is a spelling of a word whose standard accented form is ALSO in the list
# (più, perché, città, ...). wordfreq counts the web's unaccented typing as its own token, so
# the classifier kept both, and the keyboard then offered the misspelling: a swipe showed
# `perche` beside `perché`, and a typed `perche` was a dictionary word that nothing corrected.
# Rule for a drop: hunspell it_IT (LibreOffice, the oracle extract_apostrophe_words.py uses)
# rejects the bare lowercase form AND accepts the accented twin, and the bare form is not a name
# whose Capitalised form hunspell accepts. Real words that collide stay (si/sì, la/là, ne/né,
# se/sé, da/dà, di/dì, te/tè, li/lì, papa/papà, pero/però, cosi/così, meta/metà, unita/unità,
# lascio/lasciò and every other -o/-ò, -i/-ì verb pair hunspell accepts), and so do two names:
# `sara` (Sara: 69 mid-sentence capitalised uses vs 85 lowercase in the Leipzig it-2019 corpus)
# and `fara` (Fara, a place: 9 vs 8). The dictionary is lowercase-only and autocorrect keeps the
# typed capital, so dropping them would turn "Sara" into "Sarà". Foreign names typed without
# their accents (garcia, jose, lopez, pokemon, elite) are not Italian accent errors and stay.
# Full table with frequencies: docs/eval/2026-11-19-italian-accent-spellings.md.
# Pinned by src/test/kotlin/tribixbite/cleverkeys/ItalianAccentSpellingDriftTest.kt.
