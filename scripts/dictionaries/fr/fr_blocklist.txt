# Every entry below is a spelling of a word whose standard accented form is ALSO in the list

# `francais` beside `français` (CTC collision alternates, 2026-11-18), and a typed `francais` was
# a dictionary word that nothing corrected. Candidates: scripts/accent_twin_audit.py with hunspell
# fr "toutes variantes" v7.7 (Grammalecte via Debian hunspell-fr-comprehensive, so the 1990
# reform spellings apparaitre/aout/connaitre are valid and stay): no form of the word is accepted,
# lowercase or Capitalised, while a twin is. Kept on review (see
# docs/eval/2026-11-20-accent-spellings.md): words French text uses as they are (the,
# email, edit, real, beta, metal, museum, arena), the abbreviations dec/ref, compound fragments
# (pêle, aigues, aït, aïn) and indé, personal names that exist unaccented in other languages
# (adele, felix, noel, zoe), and names' own spellings (kévin, lévi). Wrong-accent forms (trés,
# déja, réglement) and place names French writes with accents (montreal, quebec) go.
